## Shared composition

Use the page-map palette and local Instrument fonts. Body text uses ink or `--swat-text-muted`; paper-light and aqua work on deep green. Clay is a decorative accent, not small body text. Display type is roman 400 with selective italic 400; supporting labels use Sans 600. Avoid tiny pseudo-cartographic labels, excessive tracking and uppercase paragraphs.

Keep body copy around 45–65 characters per line and 1.55–1.65 leading. Headlines use about 0.94 leading and modest negative tracking, with natural line breaks. Do not apply the huge display scale to captions or source titles. The home “Swat.” wordmark may be larger than the long-title scale after image review. Use thin rules to connect reading order, not boxes around every section.

Desktop photographic entries can occupy roughly 85–95svh, capped near 960 px; content must determine the minimum height. On phones, use an auto-height title/copy composition and a separate image when necessary. A missing photograph becomes an intentional typographic passage, not a large empty photo-shaped box. Caption, creator, licence and source link remain visible in normal flow. Shared footer: “Take the long way.”, a short editorial statement, real navigation and sources; no numbered chapter labels.

## Responsive art direction

These are composition targets, not screenshot-height requirements. Shared values are implemented in `src/styles/reference-tokens.css` and demonstrated in the study.

| CSS width | Gutter        | Long title / section heading / body | Composition                                                                                                                   |
| --------- | ------------- | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **320**   | 20 px         | 64 / 52 / 16 px                     | One column; headline wraps naturally. All copy before optional maps. No pinned sequence; image may retain its original ratio. |
| **390**   | 23 px         | 72 / 56 / 17 px                     | Portrait crops only after review. Mosaic and destinations become an authored vertical sequence; languages stack.              |

| **1024**  | about 47 px   | 120 / 88 / 18 px                    | Introduce the 44/56 river split, offset paragraphs and asymmetric story mosaic. Test long titles against real image subjects. |
| **1440**  | about 66 px   | 160 / 112 / 19 px                   | Full authored desktop composition. Keep text measures constrained while photographs occupy the wider field.                   |
| **1920**  | 74 px minimum | 168 / 128 / 20 px                   | Cap type and reading widths; centre the editorial canvas near 1760 px. Full-bleed photography can extend beyond it.           |

At 768 px, the quiet three-line study headline uses a smaller optical scale to preserve its phrase structure; semantic reading order remains unchanged. Mobile hero text must not require a 300 px spacer to find a caption or action. A photo shown at a different aspect ratio must receive its own reviewed crop and appropriate responsive source dimensions to avoid layout shift.
