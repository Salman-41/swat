## Limits and release boundary

Actual photographs, crop safe areas and real 1x/2x image encodings could not be reviewed or benchmarked. The success branch of the image selector has contract coverage, but no genuine photograph has passed end-to-end original download and pixel review in this environment. Real imagery must complete that path before the photographic art-direction stage can close.

The atlas has no acquired geometry or verified coordinates, and Fieldnotes has no paired satellite scenes. Contributor portraits, craft media and licensed audio remain absent. No GSAP/Lenis choreography or MapLibre rendering is implemented in this phase. Their performance, cleanup and interaction tests belong to their implementation phases.

No Lighthouse, screen-reader certification, field CWV, LCP/INP/CLS result or GPU profile is asserted. The current static scaffold cannot establish final production performance. Nothing was deployed or registered with a hosting service.
